# Hazard evidence and coverage

The study workbook contains 971 events, 912 of them non-Biological. The analysis retains each original FEMA disaster identifier. The county join uses current OpenFEMA records with matching state and incident start/end dates, and excludes statewide and unsupported county codes. The population proxy sums all matched designated counties using 2020 population from the vintage 2025 county estimates. It describes the administrative area and includes residents outside any footprint. See [coverage by feature](results/hazards/hazard_coverage.csv) and [matched county identifiers](results/hazards/county_keys.csv).

| Original label | Evidence verified or screened | Boundaries on interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flood, 198 | The FY2019–24 study of 92 cases retained NASA Daymet rainfall profiles for 87 and USGS discharge anomalies for 11. The NOAA administrative/report screen linked 157 of all 198 floods. The 2023–24 sample tested water gains, losses and seasonal controls. | Point rainfall is a gridded estimate. Gauge relevance and flood depth vary by event. No representative observed flood polygons, return periods, event-wide durations, or exposure estimates were accepted. |
| Hurricane, 108 | NHC HURDAT2 matches for 86 Hurricane declarations. Match by archived storm name, incident dates and a 250 km track-to-designated-area screen. Preserve wind kt, minimum central pressure hPa, 34-kt radius nautical miles and nearby landfall markers. | Best tracks, post-event FEMA designations and regional wind values are retrospective. Values along the track cannot establish local wind, surge or inundation. Archived advisory timing is addressed separately. |
| Fire, 33 | Verified MTBS retrieval for all 33 date/state queries. Eight name/date/perimeter matches were found across six declarations. Four single-fire links contributed complete mapped burned acreage as a feature. | Multiple named fires require union and coverage validation. Fire duration, radiative power, severity classes and affected structures remain unestimated. |
| Severe Storm, 430 | 301 with a county-linked NOAA event, 123 with a measured gust, 212 with an estimated gust, 170 with hail and 155 with a tornado EF rating. Archive unit and event-type checks separate estimated and measured gusts. | NOAA reporting practices and local event overlap can influence associations. Departmental figures are maxima among candidate matches. |
| Tornado, 27 | 25 county-linked EF ratings and path lengths/widths. NOAA event and episode IDs retained with ambiguity checks. | Path dimensions and EF do not supply complete track geometry or infrastructure impact. |
| Earthquake, 8 | ComCat catalog query uses a regional bounding box, magnitude floor and strict origin-time and epicenter-in-county screens. Two cases have a single start-date candidate with magnitude, depth and a ShakeMap MMI summary. | Aftershocks, offshore origins, and unaffected epicenter counties can change match coverage. No validated county shaking, PGA or asset metrics. |
| Snowstorm, Severe Ice Storm and Winter Storm, 68 | NOAA winter event types screened in the same county/date pipeline. Zero strict county report matches. | NWS forecast-zone records were excluded because historical zone-to-county geometry was unavailable. Snow, ice accumulation, temperature and service disruption were unavailable at the required resolution. |
| Drought, 0 | Workbook inventory confirmed no Drought label. | A drought model would require a new population with a comparable target. |
| Tropical Storm, Typhoon, Coastal Storm and other labels | Six Tropical Storm best-track matches, sparse NOAA coverage elsewhere, and no new independently verified physical measurements for the remaining small groups. | HURDAT2 basin coverage excludes western Pacific typhoons. The analysis does not pool them into a dedicated learned model or imply measured zero severity. |

The Phase 27 audit found zero US flood/storm activations for FY2019–24 in the specified Copernicus catalog and its USFIMR listing ended in 2017. The peer-reviewed Global Flood Database covers 2000–2018. OPERA and Sentinel image work established observability issues and local changes. NASA now provides historical MCDWD_L3 raw files covering 2003–2025. Two 2023 file entries were confirmed, and the raw download route responded with an access redirect. A separate public NASA GIBS rendering allowed bounded window checks. [Copernicus and USFIMR audit](../analysis/PHASE27_FLOOD_CATALOG_VALIDATED_RESULTS.md), [Global Flood Database](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03695-w), [NASA LAADS archive](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/products/MCDWD_L3).

Administrative population gives a useful coverage check. Infrastructure, economic exposure, depth and actual losses require dated asset layers and accepted event footprints. This continuation did not derive them from report locations, census totals or rendered flood colors.
