# Generator support and negative results

| Method | Observed training support and outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Cost-sensitive learning and repeats | Compared at 2×, 5×, 10× and 20×. Weights altered fitting importance. Repeats duplicated eligible training examples without changing case identity or values. Both remain exploratory controls. |
| SMOTE | The same observed nominal tuple and a distinct eligible episode were available only in the FY2024 Puerto Rico hurricane $500M+ cell. Earlier inner tuning data never supported this variant. The fixed FY2024 trials did not materially improve the matched result. |
| SMOTENC | Mixed-distance neighbors were available in the California Fire $50M–$200M cell from the FY2020 outer origin, and Florida Hurricane $50M–$200M and Puerto Rico Hurricane $500M+ in FY2024. Nominal values came from an observed joint tuple. Nested $50M+ AP fell from .350 to .245. |
| Constrained SMOTER and SMOGN | Log-dollar interpolation or small, bounded Gaussian perturbations generated in the same cells. Nested tail MAE moved by +$0.32M for SMOTER and −$0.35M for SMOGN. Both paired intervals include zero. Neither improved tail interval coverage. |
| Real–synthetic hybrid | Refit with full real-row weight and one-quarter weight for the synthetic rows. Nested tail MAE moved by −$0.014M with a paired interval spanning zero. There were no additional covered tail cases. |
| Gaussian copula | The regularized empirical-copula implementation required eight independent episodes within a state–hazard–band stratum and variation in both continuous dimensions. The support gate closed in all folds. Requested ratios replayed the reference fit and no copula draws entered training. |
| CTGAN and other flexible generators | The prespecified study gate required 100 independent compatible training episodes and enough real validation support to screen for memorization and extrapolation. The largest stratum had two episodes. No deep generator was fit. |
| Physics-informed simulation | The 21 USACE flood scenarios estimate direct damage. They do not supply independent FEMA obligation outcomes. Details appear below. |

No chronology had two same-state, same-hazard episodes for $200M–$500M. The only eligible $500M+ pair appeared in FY2024. There were at most two donors in any admissible cell for the novel methods. The 10× and 20× requests for these cells therefore used at most 5×, with identical draws for the same effective ratio. Extra copies added no independent empirical support. [Training support](results/support.csv), [trial-level registry](results/registry.csv.gz).

Every generated source key was checked against the exact eligible fold training roster. Novel draws preserve state and hazard, retain a complete observed categorical combination, stay inside real stratum bounds for declaration delay and any monetary proxy, and use a distinct second episode for interpolation or SMOGN screening. Classifier-only synthesis carries no dollar label. The held declaration, geographic footprint, exposure, observed severity and incident dates were never generated. The study rejected verbatim training-row matches for novel generators and audited repeated feature tuples, nearest real-source distance, donor concentration and Spearman delay–amount relationships. Groups with two independent episodes cannot establish a stable dependence structure. SMOGN reused a real feature vector while perturbing its proxy amount in 98 of 359 draws across all trials. This form of near-memorization was recorded and is material in interpreting the gains. [Synthetic feature rows](results/synthetic.csv.gz), [distribution and memorization diagnostics](results/generator_distributions.csv), [trial-level registry](results/registry.csv.gz).

Two sensitivity designs omit the largest eligible training episode in each origin or the most-used SMOTER episode when that method could generate. Refits reuse the original origin's random-number seed namespace while excluding the episode from training and lineage. Omission can eliminate the only admissible donor pair. Case-level predictions are retained for the same real test population. [Omission summary](results/sensitivity_summary.csv), [case-level refits](results/sensitivity.csv.gz).

## Independent flood impact pilot

Livingston Parish, Louisiana (FIPS 22063), had 66,814 structures in the public USACE National Structure Inventory payload. The 42,409 one-story residences without basements that had positive modeled structure value total $13.026B in replacement stock. The pilot used the USACE EGM 01-03 mean curves for this occupancy. Both structure and contents damage fractions use structure replacement value as the denominator. The exposure design placed 1%, 5% or 20% of the qualifying stock value at a uniform assumed first-floor depth of 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 feet. The resulting modeled direct structure and contents losses range from approximately $28.0M to $2.907B. Scenario inputs are explicit assumptions and no measured flood footprint was used. [USACE damage curves](https://planning.erdc.dren.mil/toolbox/library/EGMs/egm01-03.pdf), [USACE inventory documentation](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/nsi/technicalreferences/2026/technical-documentation), [retained exposure aggregate](inputs/physics_inventory_summary.json), [all scenarios](results/physics_impact_pilot.csv).

Direct physical damage, economic loss, humanitarian need and mission-assignment obligations represent different quantities. This pilot estimates a subset of direct economic losses. Humanitarian need was unmeasured. The scenario ledger leaves the FEMA obligation field unassigned because the study found no validated bridge from modeled property damage through federal tasking, authorizations and obligation timing. A property-loss simulation can support physical exposure analysis within those limits. No authoritative scenario-to-obligation bridge was established for flooding, hurricanes, fire or severe storms. [FEMA Mission Assignments Policy](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_policy-104-010-3_mission-assignments_2024.pdf).
