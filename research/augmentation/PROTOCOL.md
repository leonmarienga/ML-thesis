## Expected training-only comparisons

Use a fixed Extra Trees classifier and log1p-dollar regressor on the original metadata fields and an unweighted real-only reference. Compare the existing archived matched forward models for context. For each training fold compare sample weights and random repeat sampling at 2×, 5×, 10× and 20× total minority mass, including the original rows. For novel augmentation, apply the ratio within eligible training strata. Leave ineligible strata real-only and report exactly where a method can generate. The requested multiplier is a ceiling. Cap 10× at four or more independent source episodes per stratum and 20× at eight or more. Eligibility depends exclusively on the training partition.

- SMOTE uses a different episode in the same hazard, state, funding band and joint categorical tuple. Only continuous delay is interpolated. SMOTENC uses the same hazard/state/band and distance on numeric and categorical metadata. The categorical vector comes from an observed donor tuple through a categorical vote. It makes no fractional categories. Classifier-only synthetic inputs have no funding amount.
- The constrained SMOTER variant interpolates reported log1p amounts within the same funding band and hazard/state, using two different episodes and copying a valid nominal vector. SMOGN uses a within-stratum distance gate and small Gaussian perturbations only inside observed ranges, with the same distinct-episode requirement. Retain a capped hybrid giving each SMOTER synthetic record one-quarter weight with full real-row weight.
- Test Gaussian copula eligibility at eight independent source episodes within an admissible state/hazard/band stratum. Use empirical marginals and regularized rank-normal correlation if the gate passes. Record the continuous degrees of freedom and reject constant-variable strata. Set a deliberately conservative CTGAN study gate at 100 independent episodes plus enough real validation support to audit extrapolation and memorization for the target class. This is a study gate for these small strata.
- A physics impact pilot can use an authoritative depth–damage model and explicit synthetic exposure and hazard scenarios. Economic loss, human need and administrative obligations have different sources and meanings. A synthetic funding label requires a separately supported bridge to Mission Assignment obligations. Physics cases without such a bridge remain outside supervised model inputs.

## Nested selection and measurement

For each outer origin, generate separately inside two older inner tuning folds and two disjoint real calibration folds. Refit on the full eligible outer training set. Tune ratios from pooled real earlier validation scores only, prioritizing catastrophic average precision for classification and upper-tail absolute dollar error for regression when present. Record every ratio, support, donor and selected variant. Review at fixed 10% natural-prevalence catastrophe probability. Earlier real calibration predictions set a smoothed binary intercept for catastrophe probabilities and residual-based 80%/90% dollar intervals. Keep every calibration, validation and test row real. Raw probabilities and calibrated probabilities are both evaluated. Calibration data are distinct from the ratio-selection validation windows.

Report six-band accuracy, balanced accuracy, macro F1, every upper-band recall, catastrophic average precision and Brier/calibration error, alert workload and false alerts, dollar MAE/RMSE/R², tail MAE, interval width and conditional coverage. Use paired episode-cluster resampling for core differences. Inspect sensitivity to omitting the largest eligible tail episode in each training fold and to the most-used source episodes, without modifying the held-out cases or choosing methods from those results. No synthetic row counts as an independent disaster.

## Provenance and safeguards

Every generator runs on eligible training inputs only. Validate geographic and hazard identity, joint categorical support, continuous source bounds and the funding band when applicable. Preserve generation seed, source declaration and episode keys, support and rejection counts, generated-row keys and duplication, source distance and distribution diagnostics. Numeric synthesis occurs only in feature space, with no synthetic date, physical measurement, kind of FEMA transaction or real declaration identifier. Retain exact repeats only as a labeled control. Review memorization against the generating training fold. Retain failures where novelty or source support is inadequate.

## Implementation clarification

The existing per-fit episode purge applies at every origin. When earlier tuning and calibration windows shared an episode across fiscal years, exclude its calibration-window records so ratio selection and calibration use disjoint episode groups. Keep those records labeled in the inner prediction ledger. The episode-omission refits reuse the corresponding original outer random-number seed namespace so the omission comparisons retain the original randomization wherever the donor pool permits.
