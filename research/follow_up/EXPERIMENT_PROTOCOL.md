## Physical evidence

- Screen NOAA nClimGrid-Daily county precipitation for hurricane, flood and severe storm declarations. Require the complete incident date range, a strict county match and an archived final source release. Report geography, dates, missing months and full versus partial county coverage. Use declared-area maximum one-day and maximum county cumulative precipitation. County averages are gridded estimates.
- Construct a local wind exposure proxy from dated HURDAT2 quadrant radii and coordinates. Union extents across admissible fixes within the incident. Treat unreported radii as missing. Quantify modeled wind footprint overlap with designated county regions and Census population or housing at the finest verified scale available. Compare with the existing entire-county proxy. Investigate best-track coverage gaps by source geography, storm identity and exact validation gate.
- Screen additional public, authoritative wildfire incident perimeter archives. Require name, state, temporal match and a county intersection. Record the individual fire identifiers. Investigate named complexes and multiple perimeters separately. Reject uncertain county/time-only assignments. For a limited fine-spatial pilot, preserve exact source attributes and geometry.
- Screen the SPC historical event data as an additional route to tornado-path information and severe-storm report coverage. Report duplicate evidence risks, especially reuse of the NWS Storm Data reporting process.
