# Final scientific conclusion

This thesis supports a reproducible **research framework** that combines a frozen historical obligations benchmark, a multi-hazard evidence and uncertainty audit, and a separately evaluated allocation simulator. The empirical results do not establish sufficient decision-time accuracy, high-value interval coverage, or real humanitarian outcomes for operational use.

- **Track A reproduced.** The immutable six-band router matched 830 of 912 non-Biological cases (91.01%). The reported historical amount overlay reproduced MAE **$5.015M**, RMSE **$64.654M** and R² **0.8868**. Its leave-fiscal-year-out folds could use records from later fiscal years. The frozen original amount stack and the two accepted low-band specialists are preserved alongside the historical $50–200M overlay. [Archived execution](https://github.com/leonmarienga/ML-thesis/actions/runs/41397678781), [local audit](results/track_a/model_metrics.csv).
- **Phase 50 completed.** All 289 monthly reads succeeded and 67/67 Phase 49 transition triples reproduced. Forty-four anchors across 27 disasters met the 30% common-observability gate. Event gain was 0.0809 km² and placebo gain 0.0278 km² per 100 observed km². Including losses, the net paired difference was **−0.0571**, with median case difference 0. The six-window Minnesota audit found strong change in two Houston County windows, with independently rendered MODIS flood classifications in the same windows and overlapping locations. This establishes local sensor agreement and leaves full event footprint, depth, cause and exposure unresolved. [Phase 50 results](results/phase50/phase50_summary.csv), [independent window results](results/phase51/dr4722_modis_windows.csv).
- **No forward challenger supplanted the benchmark.** In matched, purged FY2019–24 evaluation on 376 records, the verified-link ablation improved raw accuracy from 51.06% to 59.57% while balanced accuracy decreased from 42.52% to 33.19%. Its MAE rose by $13,131 (paired cluster bootstrap interval −$14,818 to +$45,129). The nested metadata candidate had the strongest macro F1 among the evaluated variants (.3368) and $19.91M MAE. The nested verified-link variant achieved 63.56% accuracy and $31.26M MAE. The six-band medians, probability-weighted mixture, hazard specialists and mixes showed no persuasive improvement across both tasks. The feature-rich retrospective candidates used final-source records and had no independently untouched test set. [Forward metrics](results/forward/model_metrics.csv), [paired comparisons](results/forward/paired_event_bootstrap.csv).
- **Uncertainty differed sharply by funding range.** On 308 later cases, the nested metadata dollar-residual intervals covered 84.42% and 91.23% at nominal 80% and 90%. The 90% intervals missed all nine $50M+ cases. Log-scale pooling had wider, more unstable bounds. A nominal level alone is insufficient for high-stakes approval. [Interval results](results/uncertainty/interval_metrics.csv).
- **Allocation evidence comes from simulation.** Independently generated need and uncertainty drove the comparisons. In one constrained high-noise sequential scenario family, a vulnerability-weighted policy increased higher-vulnerability service by 9.61 percentage points at a reduction of 0.10 points in overall need served relative to the expected-efficiency policy. The risk-tilted version increased higher-vulnerability service further and had a larger total-service cost. Human reviewers choose and approve allocation objectives. [Simulation results](results/allocation/synthetic_allocation_metrics.csv).

The thesis contribution is the combination of artifact-level reconstruction, missingness-aware seasonal controls, explicit hazard-source attribution, and event-purged validation applied to the same FEMA decision-support problem. The project uses established surface-water mapping, conformal calibration and humanitarian cost methods. The literature check establishes methodological provenance and a case-specific contribution. A claim of global first use would require a systematic review. [JRC surface-water methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature20584), [Conformal prediction under shift](https://doi.org/10.1214/23-AOS2276), [Humanitarian logistics objectives](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jom.2013.06.002).

An independent confirmation requires authentic T0/T+72 source versions and mature, comparably defined label snapshots. A target-free FY2025 roster of 143 declarations is reserved for that work. Event-scale flood footprint verification, infrastructure/economic exposure and independently measured humanitarian need remain open empirical gaps. The archive supports exact continuation and thesis reporting within these limits. [Target-free roster](results/follow_up/fy2025_target_free_roster.csv), [technical guide](TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md).

The separate rare-obligation augmentation experiment preserved the existing candidate framework. Cost-sensitive learning and repeat sampling lowered $50M+ MAE in paired exploratory comparisons, with remaining errors above $500M. No novel generator established a transferable improvement, and every 80% or 90% interval missed all 13 high-funding cases. Compatibility checks left at most two independent training episodes in an admissible hazard–state–band stratum. The physics pilot provided plausible direct structure and contents loss scenarios and supplied no obligation or need labels. Independent mature outcomes and time-available observations remain the requirement for confirmation. [Augmentation results](augmentation/REPORT.md), [generator and physics audit](augmentation/GENERATOR_AUDIT.md), [FY2025 embargo](follow_up/FY2025_LOCK.md).

The next continuous batch established a source-level eligibility correction for future forward models. The 223 recorded zeros before the public ledger boundary cannot verify absence of Mission Assignment obligations. Their exclusion improved exploratory binary Brier score from .229 to .205 and reduced unweighted high-funding MAE by $5.71M. A stratified action audit documented substantial differences between eventual and elapsed-horizon targets, preserving the need for a separate maturity definition. The auxiliary earthquake trial validated synthetic transfer against seven held measured shaking episodes. Its approximately 3% error reduction fell short of the integration threshold with uncertainty spanning no gain. [Label coverage evidence](continuous/coverage/REPORT.md), [earthquake transfer](continuous/earthquake/REPORT.md).

The original-notice archive recovered 341 exact filing times and found all matched notices later than the first seven days. It supports historical provenance without supplying features for the primary early forecasts. The independent NOAA earthquake impact catalogue supplies real separate outcome measurements, but just four instrument-qualified regional earthquakes matched a numeric loss. The resulting evidence does not establish a physical-to-impact or a physical-to-obligation mapping. [Publication timing](continuous/filings/REPORT.md), [impact support](continuous/impacts/REPORT.md).

The full signed-action audit established a distinct elapsed-horizon evidence set of 324 historical declarations with published actions and the reporting window elapsed. It also exposed the sharp reduction in rare training support under a chronology-aware maturity rule. Of 331 dated cases with 36 months elapsed, 103 had monetary revisions beyond one cent by the current snapshot. The separate clean-water simulation introduced transport and inventory constraints and evaluated nonanticipating recourse. The CVaR trade-off fell short of the prespecified integration gate. [Full historical reconciliation](continuous/horizons/REPORT.md), [water recourse](continuous/water_recourse/REPORT.md).

Archived original announcements materially improved documentary early publication evidence. An original match was available for 177 of 248 applicability cases, including 166 documented within 24 hours under a civil-date issue bound. The archived modification clock qualified 77 of those cases at 24 hours. Exact signing and field-level versions still require separate verification. The physical wind pilot examined earlier forecast geometry against independent later site observations and showed a sensitivity–precision trade-off on three storms. [Original announcement evidence](continuous/announcements/REPORT.md), [tropical wind verification](continuous/winds/REPORT.md).

The complete historical page recovery materially increased administrative and physical coverage while preserving all previous records. It raised date-matched county links from 9,055 to 14,046 and complete county population coverage from 761 to 807. The separately versioned geographic matrix is now available for follow-on retrospective research. The matched forward comparisons give uncertain dollar and rare-ranking changes. Verified published fields and documented elapsed funding have zero jointly eligible prior cases in the tested origins. The independent six-storm wind extension also weakened a claim of consistent quadrant superiority. Together these results improve the evidence base and preserve the case for further prospective validation. [Complete geography](continuous/complete_geography/REPORT.md), [field availability](continuous/announcement_fields/REPORT.md), [wind extension](continuous/wind_extension/REPORT.md).

Independently timestamped original-site captures verified early texts for six older declarations. A California wildfire announcement gained substantive assistance language between captures. The result gives direct evidence that assigning all text to its visible release date can inject a later edit. Four cases is the largest observed training intersection of early text and the elapsed-label screen. The separate DOE disturbance comparison found a modest 1.60% improvement in 24-hour Brier from an equal real and synthetic distribution mixture. It missed the predefined practical threshold, and the incomplete 2024 endpoints limit the inference. These results preserve the operational evidence boundary for funding, resource allocation and humanitarian outcomes. [Historical content vintages](continuous/older_announcements/REPORT.md), [disturbance duration](continuous/disturbance_duration/REPORT.md).

The expanded documentary search verified exact early text for 31 historical cases. Training and held support still falls below the fixed minimum after applying episode separation and the elapsed funding outcome rule. Even full documentary coverage of the current mature population would supply at most three held $50M+ groups per fiscal origin. Stronger rare-event testing requires more independent comparable outcomes. The separate EAGLE-I source audit established a reproducible way to observe some county threshold trajectories. First drop and threshold status at 24 hours differ materially, and the archive cannot distinguish an omitted zero from a collection gap. The independent Mesa sample has too few and too narrowly distributed events for validation. These findings constrain future auxiliary simulation and leave the proposed operational framework at the existing research evidence level. [Documentary support limits](continuous/documentary_extension/REPORT.md), [county outage observability](continuous/county_outages/REPORT.md).

Federal audits establish substantial mission-assignment activity before the public obligations dataset begins. The records identify specific older declarations and show large within-agency obligation reductions. They mix snapshots across hazards, reimbursement claims and other accounting stages, and no retained document supplies the complete signed 36-month declaration history. The separate sustained outage experiment used two later chronological origins and 2,139 complete held county windows. A fixed real-only logistic comparator improved descriptive Brier compared with prevalence in both origins. Adding empirically simulated trajectories produced no reliable gain. Excluding explicit zeros with unresolved provenance led to the same decision. [Federal accounting evidence](continuous/precoverage_documentary/REPORT.md), [sustained outage evaluation](continuous/sustained_outage/REPORT.md).

The recipient-side audit recovered two more exact Katrina assignment keys and distinguished a late closure directive, a closed recipient's incurred zero, an independent interagency agreement and FEMA authorization. The component perspective still cannot support a complete elapsed declaration amount. The county wind analysis matched earlier-valid archived forecasts to observed outage entries using a preexisting geographic release. Date-only matching retained 86 distant entries. Seven complete histories fell within the regional screen and three were within the two forecast contours. These observations establish source linkage and reveal insufficient independent conditions for a wind-driven trajectory test. [Component archives](continuous/component_accounting/REPORT.md), [wind exposure support](continuous/county_wind_exposure/REPORT.md).

The exact public precoverage screen recovered zero comparable funding outcomes for five historical declarations. The target remains a complete signed, declaration-level elapsed obligation history. A geography-first hurricane panel increased authentic regional recovery support to 330 complete windows. The available independent held groups still fall below the generator gate. Separate survey evidence quantifies displaced adults' food and water shortages and post-storm household need and service receipt. These measures support empirical plausibility checks for separate humanitarian scenarios, while a lack of matched allocation and delivery evidence prevents effectiveness claims. [Ledger evidence](continuous/legacy_ledger_gate/REPORT.md), [wind and recovery evidence](continuous/storm_outage_panel/REPORT.md), [humanitarian evidence](continuous/humanitarian_source/REPORT.md), [backlog](continuous/BACKLOG.md).
