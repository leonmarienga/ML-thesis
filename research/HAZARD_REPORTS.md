## Earthquake

Original support: 8 records across 6 fiscal years and 6 state/territory labels.

## Fire

Original support: 33 records across 14 fiscal years and 10 state/territory labels.

33 records. Official MTBS program/direct-download documentation accessible; advertised map service503 and one guessed occurrence-file route404. Perimeters, acreage, severity and event linkage were not ingested. This is incomplete acquisition, not evidence that authentic wildfire data do not exist.

## Shared limitations

NOAA physical-field availability was inspected for 2024 only; no full-cohort physical coverage estimate is inferred. Population/building/road exposure was not computed from unvalidated flood candidates. Current-source metadata and final products do not certify declaration-time availability. New predictive integration remains gated on source linkage and untouched evaluation.
