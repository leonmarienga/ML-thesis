# Original design and data lineage

This crosswalk distinguishes planned features, verified archive behavior, and results that the study does not establish.

| Record | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Django study proposal, June 2026, unpublished project record, §§3.2–3.8 | The proposed phased dollar-budget study presented five cases per page, with a notional $8B total and $900M initially released. It planned an initial human allocation, advice from a two-stage XGBoost model and Clean DQN adjustment, then a final human allocation. The proposed decision log distinguished the initial choice, base prediction, multiplier and advice, final choice, balance, time and model versions. | The deployed settings, recruitment, collected logs, usability findings, or any causal benefit of assistance. A proposal to record decisions is not a participant dataset. |
| [Frozen RL notebook](../../reinforcement_learning.ipynb) and [implementation crosswalk](SIX_BAND_METHODOLOGY.md#dqn-was-an-allocation-experiment-not-the-archived-router) | The implementation and simulated evaluation of multiplier policies under a capped dollar budget. See Chapter 4 and Appendix D for its actual evaluation and reward. | The eventual six-band router's logic, optimized humanitarian service or an observed human comparison. Proposal parameters are not used to fill gaps in the execution record. |
| Later OpenFEMA extraction reconstruction note, August 2026, unpublished project record | A separate derivation used Disaster Declarations Summaries v2 and Mission Assignments v1, aggregated by disaster number and recreated the 45-column analytical structure. The note reports that the first workbook's query date, raw filters, join rules and expected-resource mapping were not all retained. | Exact historical API responses, a byte-for-byte rebuilt original workbook, or equivalence with the subsequent v2 signed-action ledger. |
| [Frozen historical evidence](../TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md) and [clean replay](replay/BASELINE_CLEAN_REPLAY_REPORT.md) | The preserved inputs and outputs support a reproducible archived calculation under its recorded conventions, including the 830/912 outcome-conditioned routing count. | End-to-end regeneration of the original extraction from live APIs, genuinely target-blind historical validation, or equivalence to prospective service demand. |
| [Prospective service tabletop](tabletop/PROTOCOL.md) and [offline software viewer](decision_support_v2/README.md) | The tabletop protocol separates unaided decisions, a frozen optimizer and assisted choices on a single-dispatch service task. The viewer is an offline way to inspect existing evidence. | The legacy phased-dollar experiment, collected participant evidence, ethics approval, or real relief effectiveness. |

The unpublished design records are retained separately and are not redistributed here. Their language is treated as historical design intent; quantitative implementation claims use the frozen code, replay or tabulated results.
