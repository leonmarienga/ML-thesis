    What these panels do not measure

    Historical FEMA obligations are accounting amounts, not requests for aid, disaster losses, or observed relief delivered. The simulations report service ratios, not dollars. The proportional rule and veto are programmed algorithms, not actual human participants or observations of human judgment. No participant study, human–AI effectiveness estimate, or field validation is shown. Neither simulation should be used to dispatch aid.

    Data provenance and reproducibility

    Builds use the frozen history and Batch 14 data at the pinned research commit, and aggregate outputs of the separate matched-scenario fixture. Historical rows and logged synthetic actions are not copied into the viewer.
