    Matched simulation • 48 new draws from the Batch 14 generator

    Which trade-offs follow from a scripted veto?

    Choose an arm, a comparison arm, and optionally a road condition. The two arms are always shown on the same simulated worlds. Three arms were evaluated: a proportional rule, a vulnerability-weighted linear optimizer, and the same optimizer with a fallback to the proportional rule if its worst eligible zone loses more than 10 points.

    Simulated servicePolicyComparisonGap (pp)Scenario interval (pp)

    Gap = policy minus comparison. Scenario intervals are archived paired resampling results for all 48 draws only, not clinical, human, or real-world effect intervals. Subgroup means are descriptive. Closed zones stay in the scored denominators.

    What these panels do not measure

    Historical FEMA obligations are accounting amounts, not requests for aid, disaster losses, or observed relief delivered. The simulations report service ratios, not dollars. The proportional rule and veto are programmed algorithms, not actual human participants or observations of human judgment. No participant study, human–AI effectiveness estimate, or field validation is shown. Neither simulation should be used to dispatch aid.

    Data provenance and reproducibility

    Builds use the frozen history and Batch 14 data at the pinned research commit, and aggregate outputs of the separate matched-scenario fixture. Historical rows and logged synthetic actions are not copied into the viewer.
