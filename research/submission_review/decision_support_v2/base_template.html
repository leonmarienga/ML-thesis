Survey anchors informed simulated household demand. Route conditions, storage and resupply were simulated assumptions. The service floor applies only to reachable, stocked request cells; closed routes still count in the coverage denominators.

Overall: delivered service divided by demand. High vulnerability: that fraction in the designated high-vulnerability zones. Least served: the minimum final service fraction across zones with demand in each case. Displayed outcomes average these fractions across the shown cases.
