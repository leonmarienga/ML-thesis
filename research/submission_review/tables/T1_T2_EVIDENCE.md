# T1 source coverage and T2 cohort trace

- [T1 selected source coverage](T1-source-coverage.csv) comes from the [frozen hazard coverage CSV](../../results/hazards/hazard_coverage.csv) and [source feature manifest](../../SOURCE_AND_FEATURE_MANIFEST.csv). The only aggregate in the extract is county population: sum the `designated_2020_population` column over the 20 incident types (805) and the `total` column (971). For every other row, select its `incidentType` and the appropriate nonmissing count: `cyclone_max_wind_kt`, `mtbs_burned_acres`, `quake_magnitude`, `measured_gust_kt`, `tornado_ef`, `daymet_point_max_24h_mm`, or `usgs_discharge_anomaly`. Counts indicate audit linkage, not raw source completeness, authentic affected population or availability as of a past decision.
- [T2 evaluation populations](T2-validation-cohorts.csv) cross-references the [frozen outcomes and conventions](../../results/track_a/model_metrics.csv), [forward split evidence](../figures/F3-forward-split-audit.svg), [target-blind trial](https://github.com/leonmarienga/ML-thesis/tree/6bd74e7dceaf339df2b9f911141a8a7281428d5f/research/followup_20261128/target_blind_router_v1), [signed cohort and observability audit](../../continuous/coverage_specialists/REPORT.md) and [matched interval scope](../figures/uncertainty/REPORT.md). The 90-day buffer governs eligibility, not the 36-month action sum. See Appendix D E2 for the comparison at a snapshot versus at a historical origin.
- The 912-row source population is partitioned in the signed observability audit into 223 precoverage, 313 no published action rows, one unusable date, 51 not elapsed and 324 elapsed/key-complete. The 324 partition into 27 documented signed zeros and 297 positives. Neither a no-action result nor a precoverage historical workbook zero justifies an absence-of-obligation claim.
- Source version for frozen files: `b0b6edb229d112dd6d11896a512bf1112414e4f6`. These are evidence extracts and a trace of evaluation definitions, not new model fits, promoted model rankings or validation with untouched outcomes.
