# Academic review and submission boundaries

The English supervisor-review manuscript and reproducibility materials are not a university submission. The [graduate regulations, Article 19](https://www.eul.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/1-Regulations_GraduateSchool-2.pdf#page=10), place thesis writing and defense in the programme language, assign formatting specifications to the Institute, and describe supervisor acceptance before the formal defense application. These sources were checked on 28 November 2026.

The review cover uses a working author display name. Before preparing the submission copy, confirm the current Institute template, the author name and order in the university record, supervisor details, whether Turkish front matter is required, signed declaration and AI-disclosure wording, and the formal submission method. The public programme and regulation establish neither the exact template nor the separate abstract requirement. No approval, signature, or academic declaration has been supplied on the author's behalf.

The human-only, algorithm-only, and human–AI study remains prospective. Dry-run outputs reflect scripted choices on synthetic scenarios. No participants were enrolled and there is no behavioral result. Obtain institutional and supervisor approval before recruiting participants, accessing private responses, or representing dry runs as a human evaluation.
