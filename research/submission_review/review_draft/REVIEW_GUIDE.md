# Supervisor review guide

Start with the editable supervisor-review draft. It brings together six chapters and three appendices, embeds the historical error-concentration, matched interval and transport-frontier figures, and records which claims are historical, simulated, or still unverified. This is not a graduate-school-format certification or a signed submission.

1. Review Chapters 1–3 for the problem definition and the distinction between archived route agreement, chronological forecasts, and uncertainty. The 91.01% historical metric is not a target-blind test.
2. In Chapter 4, inspect the clearly labeled research-only amount overlay, unpromoted target-blind candidate, failed extreme interval coverage, and the coverage–equity trade-off on synthetic scenarios. Do not infer aid delivered, actual participants, or deployment validity.
3. Download the read-only `../decision_support_v2/index.html` and open it in a browser. Compare the nominal levels, three scripted/optimization arms and original frontier. The browser acceptance log records the two viewport and keyboard tests; no login or upload is needed to use the viewer.
4. See Appendices A–C and the repository checklist for evidence tracing, reproduction commands, and a future participant-study protocol. The participant study was not conducted and should not delay review of the current simulation thesis.
5. Review blockers explicitly: the exact Batch 5 and 11 local comparison discrepancies, limited independently verified catastrophe and coastal evidence, manuscript citation/formatting and institutional disclosure confirmation, and final expanded-package reproducibility. Do not remove the FY2025 embargo or change the frozen evidence to close a checklist item.

The review copy is for Leon's use. It has not been transmitted to a supervisor or institution.
