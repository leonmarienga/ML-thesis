# Historical FEMA action records and the exact-declaration gate

The audit used exact major declaration identities for Katrina (Louisiana DR-1603 and Mississippi DR-1604), Gustav (Louisiana DR-1786), Ike (Texas DR-1791) and Irene (New York DR-4020). All five dates precede October 1, 2012. Each allowlisted OpenFEMA v2 request returned a complete zero-row response. The published data scope explains why no eligible action history was recovered. An empty response for an out-of-scope declaration does not establish a zero obligation. [Locked design](../BATCH10_PROTOCOL.md), [exact query audit](results/legacy_queries.csv), [preservation catalogue metadata](https://www.datalumos.org/datalumos/project/218462/version/V1/view).

Two already retained postcoverage Sandy declarations provide a route control. The validated historical snapshot contains 1,548 keyed actions, including 645 negative actions, across DR-4085 and DR-4086. These controls are two declarations in one storm group and represent existing evidence. Their records demonstrate that the public schema can represent negative actions within its advertised coverage. They do not certify recovery of any precoverage originals or prove a past public release date for each row. [Retained route controls](results/retained_control.csv), [signed horizon audit](../horizons/REPORT.md).

The archived FEMA policy describes activation, amendments and the later reduction of unliquidated obligations when billings and closeouts are incomplete. OMB guidance describes recipient-side support for obligations, outlays and reimbursement through the Disaster Finance Center. The audited Ike program report identifies the use of a mission assignment for carcass disposal. That task record does not furnish all signed actions, all agency coverage or a complete 36-month record. These sources identify authorities and stages without supplying comparable declaration totals. [FEMA policy](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_policy-104-010-3_mission-assignments_2024.pdf), [OMB financial guidance](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/omb/assets/omb/financial/memos/disaster_relief_091707.pdf), [GAO Ike evidence](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-11-652.pdf).

The direct FEMA policy download was blocked and an exact public archive copy was retained. The RegInfo collection returned a service error. The preservation catalogue was inspected as metadata because a broad copy can include embargoed financial outcomes. NARA's Senate access rules identify applicable record-vintage and disclosure limits. They do not confirm access to the particular underlying signed forms. No further public source from this route supplied a complete 36-month ledger. Zero new monetary labels passed. A new fit would require original dated signed actions, linked amendments and offsets, exact declaration identities and proof of complete scope at the common horizon. [Documentary route manifest](results/documentary_routes.csv), [NARA access rules](https://www.archives.gov/legislative/research/rules-of-access).
