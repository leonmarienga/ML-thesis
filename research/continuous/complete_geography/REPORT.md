# Authenticated full-page administrative geography

The original geography reader accepted only filenames with a one-digit offset. This limited the historical processed release to 15 offset-zero pages. The working source cache supplied all 36 pages already referenced and hashed in the original source manifest. All pages passed exact byte, fiscal-year, offset and full-count verification. The complete release has 29,869 records, including 15,423 after the first pages. The added records include overlapping dates, multiple administrative designations and Biological cases. Source-row count is distinct from declaration and county coverage. [Pagination audit](results/pagination_audit.csv), [archived source manifest](../../inputs/RAW_SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv).

The replay incorporated full pages while preserving the original state, incident timing, FIPS and title logic. For the 912 non-Biological cases, dated county matches grew from **9,055 to 14,046**, with added scope on 544 cases. The number with any usable county rose from 809 to 860. All original date-locked county instances remain included. Severe Storm links increased from 4,095 to 6,122, Flood from 1,896 to 2,841, Hurricane from 1,595 to 2,663 and Fire from 146 to 322. The change is an administrative-data coverage correction. County designations are distinct from verified hazard footprints. [Case coverage](results/county_coverage_by_declaration.csv), [recovered county keys](results/county_keys.csv).

Replaying the original hazard assembly raised complete county population coverage from 761 to 807. Coverage of linked measured gust rose from 123 to 145, estimated gust from 212 to 232, tornado EF from 180 to 199, cyclone wind from 92 to 101 and reviewed wildfire burned acreage from four to five. Join evidence retains original attribution rules. These changes measure coverage and can include added linkage candidates. They do not establish an early version or a validated full-event exposure. [Replayed coverage](hazard_replay/hazard_coverage.csv), [versioned feature matrix](hazard_replay/cohort_features.csv).

## Matched forward replay

Both versions used 376 identical validation cases, the same precoverage label exclusion and the union of both episode relations as a shared purge. The fixed verified physical and geometry model had 59.31% raw accuracy in both versions. Balanced accuracy rose from 32.17% to 44.35%, with a paired interval of −16.34 to +21.28 percentage points for the change. The recovered model routed the single $200M–$500M held declaration correctly and lost the previous correct classifications in the $50M–$200M and $500M+ bands. Upper-band evidence is sparse. Tail average precision changed from .401 to .406. Overall MAE changed from $19.62M to $19.50M, and tail MAE from $547.96M to $544.13M. Paired changes for overall MAE (−$323,579 to +$23,416) and tail MAE (−$9.51M to +$0.13M) remain uncertain. [Metrics](ablation/metrics.csv), [episode-paired comparisons](ablation/paired.csv).

The complete geographic source version is retained as the retrospective input for future separately versioned candidates. The exploratory predictions do not replace Track A or establish decision-time improvement.
