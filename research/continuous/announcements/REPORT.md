# Timestamped original major-disaster announcements

Official archived White House announcements provide a materially earlier public route than the Federal Register notices. They offer an evidence path for T+24 and T+48 feature reconstruction and leave exact T0 and complete field-level vintage unresolved.

The historical-only search screened 245 pre-October 2024 original-looking approval URLs from the 13 archived post sitemaps. A matched date and UTC offset from both Open Graph and JSON-LD, an original major disaster statement, and an unambiguous historical state, declaration day and incident start established 177 exact bindings among 248 applicable cases. The remaining documents comprise 53 with unresolved metadata or text structure and 15 that did not yield a unique case binding. The matching uses no funding target. The earlier clock documents publication of an article. The other clock is known for 91 matched records and all 91 are subsequent to publication. Another 86 matched records lack an unambiguous last-modification timestamp. Exact agreement of two archive metadata fields establishes consistent authoring metadata. It is not an independent timestamp record of users' first access or proof that the archived text matches the initial version. The outcome-free state and incident binding could support later program and geography extraction. Initial program and county contents require version-specific checks before being counted as available. The effective declaration date still does not identify its signing instant.
