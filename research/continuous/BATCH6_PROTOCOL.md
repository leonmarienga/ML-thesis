# Batch 6 protocol

## Authentic data and forecast workstream

The earlier White House archive is a route to dated program and jurisdiction descriptions before 2021. Test a bounded, outcome-free archive sample before expanding retrieval. Use the official 2017–2021 archive index and one briefing sitemap per calendar quarter from 2017–2020, beginning in January, April, July and October. Identify presidential approval or declaration pages by their public slugs. Inspect up to two pages per selected month in lexicographic order and retain the uninspected candidate URLs. Pace official archive requests at least ten seconds apart, as specified by its robots file.

Match an announcement only when the state or territory, declared civil day and incident onset match a unique historical FEMA case. Keep visible release dates and sitemap dates separate from timezone-resolved publication and last-modification clocks. A same-day release claim gives a conservative civil-day bound on the claimed release. It cannot authenticate the current page text as the version available that day. Permit early field evidence only when an explicitly marked modification timestamp or an independently dated copy establishes that the inspected content existed by the decision horizon. Do not convert sitemap modification, HTTP headers or archive discovery time into an original content clock. Compute both lower and upper feasibility bounds by fiscal origin using training-only published signatures and the elapsed 36-month plus 90-day maturity gate. Historical matched identities with an unresolved version provide an upper bound only. Repeat with a coarser population bound over all historical eligible declarations.

## Synthetic and measured-outcome workstream

Use weather-related electricity disturbance reports as a separate, observed service endpoint. First inspect the primary DOE and EIA summary schemas. Use the reported end of qualifying disturbance (elapsed hours), with strict validity checks on duration and no assumed zeros. The end of a reportable event is not a household outcome or a measure of humanitarian need. Restrict acquisition to calendar years 2018–2024 and model events through September 2024. Use no cause other than weather and natural hazards. Record source changes and missing reporting times. Delay fit and synthetic generation until the source, feature and episode definitions are fixed. If the compact DOE or EIA summaries fail the source gate, inspect the published EAGLE-I files and require a bounded extraction that preserves missing versus observed zero. Both experiments leave FY2025 and all frozen inputs untouched.

### Locked disturbance experiment

The public DOE annual summaries provide 2018–2024 spreadsheets with onset and restoration clocks. Harmonize the two header layouts and retain reports whose event type explicitly includes “Severe Weather” or “Weather or natural disaster” and whose criteria include loss of electric service to more than 50,000 customers. This selects reports, not all outages or customers. Preserve a record of missing or malformed end times and limit scored reports to strictly positive elapsed civil-clock durations. Exclude events beginning on or after October 1, 2024. Exclude recorded 2024 restoration times beyond that date as unavailable labels at the study cutoff. Treat a future clock as unavailable even if published in the annual summary. The summaries omit per-row timezones and actual reporting vintages, so treat elapsed durations as local civil-clock approximations. Assess threshold robustness by excluding observations from 23 through 25 hours in a sensitivity calculation.

For 2021–2024 forward origin years, omit the most recent prior calendar year to approximate annual publication lag. Require both onset and restoration at least 14 days before the omitted year. Create fixed calendar-time blocks from onset dates for paired uncertainty analysis, using 14-day blocks as primary and 28-day blocks as sensitivity. These blocks are dependence proxies rather than adjudicated meteorological episodes. Use only meteorological season derived from the onset date as a forecast feature. The weather and customer-loss criteria define the research population and are based on final reports. No reported customer counts, load loss, restoration data or post-event geographical fields will enter the forecasting features.

Compare a pooled empirical distribution, an empirical distribution by season with pooled fallback below eight observations, a log-normal model whose season log-mean and variance are shrunk toward the past pooled statistics with a fixed weight of 20, and a 50–50 mixture of the empirical season distribution and the parametric simulation. Generate 1,024 midpoint probability-grid draws per origin and season. Synthetic draws represent a modeling prior and do not add independent observations. Report 24-hour Brier score, one-sided 90% coverage and log-duration pinball loss, medians, abstention and missingness. For promotion of the simulated mixture over the empirical seasonal baseline require a minimum 5% relative Brier improvement, both block confidence intervals excluding no improvement, empirical upper coverage of at least 85%, and no increase in 90% pinball loss. Treat the reporting-rule population and any measured benefit as distinct from allocation, household restoration, and FEMA amounts.
