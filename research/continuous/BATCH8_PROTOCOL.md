# Batch 8 protocol

## Authentic records and obligation comparability

Audit pre-October-2012 federal documentary traces for Hurricanes Katrina and Rita and a second precoverage disaster or reporting cohort if authoritative sources support it. Include the official GAO Katrina preliminary report and the DHS Inspector General's earlier mission-assignment account. Review up to eight additional official reports found through explicit mission-assignment references, agency product searches or links from these records. Save the original documents, hashes and itemized evidence extracts. Read for accounting terms, disaster scope and dates before extracting dollar amounts. Preserve fiscal or agency aggregates, individual assignments, authorizations, payments, damage estimates and any signed amounts as separately classified evidence. A comparable new training label requires an exact declaration match, dated signed actions with amendment and cancellation coverage, a complete 36-month endpoint, and clear scope for observed zero or ledger absence. A local or agency subtotal supplies context only. Count all gaps and test whether any authentic funding labels pass. Neither workbook outcomes nor FY2025 outcomes will be updated or queried.

## Synthetic and observed outage trajectories

Retain the 24 counties and the observation validity rules from Batch 7. Use the frozen 2018 subset and the author-released 2020, 2022 and 2023 national files from Figshare version 2, with a 3.6 GB national transfer cap and integrity verification against published MD5. Retain verified exact selected rows and source metadata for offline replay. Require an observed below-1,000 count immediately before crossing to at least 1,000. For each county keep the first qualifying entry and skip subsequent entries strictly within 48 hours of the last retained entry. Make this decision even when the eventual outcome is censored. The primary endpoint is all of the final 12 successive quarter-hour counts below 1,000 in a complete 24-hour window. Record outcome absence separately and never fill missing counts with zero. County windows are observed trajectories and can include multiple utilities or disturbances.

Hold calendar 2022 and 2023 out in turn. Use only observations from earlier calendar years for model fit, scaling, generation and interval analysis inputs. Require all training label follow-up to end at least 14 days before the origin and never score a window beyond its source year. Each held origin must have at least 50 complete windows, both classes with at least 20 outcomes and at least 12 occupied 14-day blocks. Use real complete windows only in test. Treat 14-day and 28-day UTC blocks as dependence proxies. The model conditions on eventual observation of a complete window even though completeness is unknown when forecasting. Archived utility observations lack independently timestamped historical public releases for each row. This is chronological retrospective transfer for the measured endpoint and no operational public-warning claim.

Compare pooled prevalence and a fixed standardized L2 logistic model using log1p onset and predecessor counts, their log1p increment, and sine and cosine of the onset day within year. Validate a synthetic model that uses training-only empirical quarter-hour count transitions in the bins 0–249, 250–499, 500–999, 1,000–1,999, 2,000–4,999, 5,000–9,999, 10,000–49,999 and 50,000 or greater. Fit separate transition estimates for steps 1–4, 5–16 and 17–96. Shrink each state row toward its pooled across-phase empirical transitions with fixed mass 20. An unobserved outgoing state holds its current state. Pooling may only generate observed state pairs. The simulation represents counts within bins and is an empirical auxiliary generator. Generate eight trajectories per eligible real training onset with seed 80719 plus origin year, using only that onset's real covariates. Give each synthetic trajectory weight 0.25/8, so the total synthetic weight equals one quarter of the real training weight. Refit the same logistic learner with this additional weighted probability information.

Report Brier, log loss, average precision, calibration in fixed probability intervals and separate results by origin. Record transition support, generated class distributions, complete-case selection and synthesis constraints. Use paired 14- and 28-day block bootstrap intervals for the hybrid Brier change, stratified by origin. Minimum promotion criteria are a pooled 5% relative Brier improvement versus matched real-only logistic, favorable 95% paired intervals under both block sizes, and no origin Brier increase greater than 0.005. The study retains meaningfully different endpoints and no proxy mapping from electricity observations to obligations or humanitarian need.
