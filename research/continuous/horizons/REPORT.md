# Signed obligations and historical maturity

The complete post-coverage historical audit used only 689 exact FY2013–24 major-declaration IDs. The public snapshot had 20,352 signed actions in 35 pages. A total of 375 declarations had dated unique action and amendment keys, 313 had no published rows, and one had an action without an obligation date. The action-free cases remain unknown for the standardized target. The public source's expected coverage and a zero sum are distinct. [Action source manifest](results/action_source_manifest.csv), [declaration-level audit](results/historical_horizons.csv).

The current signed sum differed from the original workbook by over one cent in 51 of the 375 cases with dated, structurally usable actions. The original outcome remains frozen for historical reproduction. For the separately defined elapsed-horizon audit, 324 cases had eligible action structure and had passed the 36-calendar-month window and 90-day reporting buffer at retrieval. These are elapsed retrospective quantities. The public files provide no archived version from the historical model origin and do not certify that a public absence is an observed zero.

The 36-month comparison counts declarations where 36 months alone have elapsed. Applying the added reporting buffer gives the smaller set of 324. Across the 331-case comparison, no cases crossed the $50M, $200M or $500M thresholds between the 36-month sum and the current archive. Dollar amounts can still change considerably within a band. At 12 months four threshold crossings appeared over the three boundaries and the maximum monetary change was much larger. The denominators differ across horizons. ## Historical origin limits

With the previous event/date purges plus 36 months and 90 days elapsed before the safe origin cutoff, the FY2019 fit would have 68 dated public-action rows and one independent $50M+ episode. For FY2024 the corresponding counts are 230 rows and 12 episodes. The first three origins have zero $500M+ historical training episodes under this rule. The last three have two each. At no maturity-screened origin does a compatible same-state, same-hazard $200M–$500M or $500M+ stratum have two independent training episodes. The only compatible pairs in the matured audit occur in the $50M–$200M band. Copula and deep synthetic gates therefore remain closed. [Past-only support and generator feasibility](results/past_only_support.csv).

The label audit supports freezing a distinct fixed-horizon research endpoint once capture and vintage completeness are documented. A model trained only on the known public-action records would apply to a selected population. A complete prospective evaluation would also need reasoned treatment of declarations whose public actions are missing and authentic decision-time features. No model was fit to the standardized outcome in this batch. No FY2025 funds were queried and the existing lock remains in place. [FY2025 evaluation lock](../../follow_up/FY2025_LOCK.md).
