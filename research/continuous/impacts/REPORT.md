# Independent earthquake impact support screen

The NOAA NCEI consequential-earthquake catalogue supplies independent source records for separate damage, building and human-impact tasks. A controlled quantitative match to the regional ground-motion pilot found too little joint support to fit a physical-to-impact transfer model.

The official historical query returned 1,477 consequential events during 1998–2024. It includes 275 earthquake-only positive dollar damage reports, 419 positive numeric destroyed-housing records, and 611 positive numeric death counts. One hundred percent of populated values for these fields were positive. The catalogue's selection and measurement process includes reporting thresholds. Blanks cannot stand in for zero, physical safety or demand. The USA row has 62 consequential records, with 17 earthquake-only dollar amounts, 11 destroyed-housing counts and eight death counts. Global, USA, and earthquake-region coverage represent different statistical populations. The data keep reported millions of dollars, counts, source-defined order classes and estimated total effects separate. Cross-year comparisons would require reference, currency and inflation checks plus a defensible treatment of event selection. [NOAA metadata](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ngdc.mgg.hazards%3AG012153%3Bview%3Diso), [numeric coverage by country](results/impact_coverage.csv), [source manifest](results/source_manifest.csv).

Twelve of 28 regional USGS event candidates had one NOAA link under the prespecified day, distance, magnitude and clock rules. Distinct source event IDs are not necessarily independent sequences. The two Ridgecrest source matches belong to the same episode. Among the 12 instrument-qualified regional earthquake episodes, four had a numeric earthquake-only monetary damage report with a unique corresponding NOAA event. Three had a destroyed-housing count and three had a numeric death count. The selected sample is too sparse for either the training or forward validation gate. Recorded PGA describes individual sites. Even within those four cases, it does not quantify full-region exposure or demonstrate a causal or predictive link to reported losses. [Matched impact evidence](results/comcat_impact_links.csv), [instrument support](../earthquake/inputs/earthquake_sources.csv).

The retained global catalogue supports a future outcome selection and provenance audit. Useful next data would pair consistently defined observed impacts with independently validated building or population exposure and hazard information in the same domain. No missing loss was filled with synthetic damage, and neither recorded impacts nor modeled ground motion supplied FEMA funding or humanitarian-demand labels.
