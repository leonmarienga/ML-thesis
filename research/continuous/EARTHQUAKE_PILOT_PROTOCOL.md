The held target here is the geometric mean of two measured horizontal PGA channels in units of standard gravity, with physics-model values and field reports excluded. Product editions are retrospective and do not establish an as-of forecast.

The regional event screen uses USGS ComCat for 2005 through September 2024, 32–42.1°N and 125–114°W, reported magnitude 5.5–7.3, maximum depth 20 km and a ShakeMap product.
