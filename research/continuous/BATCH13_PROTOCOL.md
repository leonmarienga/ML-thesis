# Batch 13 protocol

## Fixed catastrophic weighting and influence

Freeze the existing signed-elapsed 20× ExtraTrees configuration and predictions. Preserve the original matched real-only comparator. Audit robustness using the same 135 cases strictly as an exploratory influence diagnostic. For each eligible historical origin, remove the single highest, second-highest or third-highest earlier tail event group by maximum eligible signed label, fitting each omission separately. Weighting, endpoint maturity, episode purge, predictors and other settings stay fixed. Run the corresponding real-only model on every same reduced training split. Use equal episode and equal declaration aggregates. Report the minimum and maximum observed differences and bootstrap the original paired episode differences. Do not use these cases to pick an omission, threshold, model, or deployment rule. Requirements for integration include independently observed mature signed actions, reproducible prediction-time sources, uncertainty validation, clearly defined allowance for overall error and false alerts, and human review. Historical labels excluded by scope stay unavailable.

## New observed coastal transfer

Fix all event windows and sites in the accompanying panel. Select by NOAA coastal gauges, approximate coastline direction, named storm geography and time. Include two earlier Batch 12 storms for training only, six additional earlier storms and four new held endpoint storms in chronological 2024. Data are independent by storm and station may repeat. Use the same three UTC dates and 72-hour grid as Batch 12. Request verified hourly height and astronomical tide predictions in local MSL and meters and exact hourly station pressure and wind in metric units. Store all provider responses and request identities. Cap at 96 initial public requests, one retry per transient error and 2 MB per response. No outcome-dependent station substitution.

Require both stations to meet 54 of 72 independently measured level and tide hours per qualifying storm. A storm must additionally have at least one site with 24 shared eligible prediction origins at both one and six hours using pressure, wind and three-hour prior history. The observation gate requires four earlier and two held storms. Compute a separate nonzero water-level flag sensitivity. The eligible information set at issue time uses water level at that time, prior water level and contemporary and earlier meteorology. Evaluate on verified retrospective water levels with unreconstructed preliminary availability. Comparisons use matched one- and six-hour origins and storm-level performance. Storms in the 2024 held set supply no fitting or tuning data.

Prespecified arms are the astronomical tide, residual persistence, real-only ridge, hydrostatic equilibrium relaxation, synthetic-only ridge initialization and hydrostatic synthetic prior plus real ridge correction. The hydrostatic benchmark conservatively holds the pressure anomaly constant and exponentially relaxes the observed level residual toward the inverse-barometer equilibrium on a nine-hour timescale. Use 1 cm sea-level rise per hPa pressure drop for this simplified reference. It cannot quantify local hydrodynamics. The synthetic arm draws 12,000 physically bounded examples per horizon with uniform pressure anomaly from −65 to +15 hPa and signed wind-stress proxy from −625 to +625 square meters per squared second. Draw tenable generic wind-response coefficients and pressure and wind histories and vary relaxation from 3 to 18 hours. Add Gaussian mismatch with standard deviation 0.10 m. Fit a fixed linear ridge to those examples. The real-only and hybrid use the same predictors, weighting storms equally and holding regularization fixed. A linear correction to the synthetic initial predictions is the hybrid. Directions are coarse proxies. Full hydrodynamic transfer with physics-grounded site bathymetry remains a separate gate. Retain the hydrostatic and stress simulation as idealized process experiments. No forcing values after the prediction clock, storm-specific fitted offsets or synthetic obligations enter the evaluation.

## Transport-aware multi-objective allocation

Use newly seeded simulated request and planning populations with the two Batch 11 CASPER survey contexts, two demand distributions, service identity, and shelter sensitivity. Predefine 12 held cases per context/distribution combination for 48 total. Test a 0%, 10%, …, 100% geographic floor grid relative to a feasible proportional stage benchmark, and prespecify overall, vulnerability, max-min and mixed preferences. Study both single-stage and sequential expected, robust worst-branch and CVaR decision objectives. Pair policies using the same simulated demand, supply and disruption. Represent one shared depot, eight zone corridors and abstract cargo weights and handling volumes by service. Restrict dispatch with resource-specific stocks, service-level delay and existing inventory, shared storage capacity, route capacity and departure-time or arrival-time loss. Bound demands, allocations, and storage. Report what a zero-capacity route does to guarantees, efficient frontiers in observed simulation, and performance under each disruption level, without claiming real policy effectiveness. Preserve seeds, assumptions and any negative trade-offs.
