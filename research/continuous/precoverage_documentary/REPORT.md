## What the dollar figures measure

The DHS Inspector General recorded approximately 2,700 issued mission assignments totaling about **$8.7B** for the Katrina response. The testimony discusses assignment tracking and closeout problems. It supplies no declaration split, signed annual action sequence or net balance at the proposed elapsed horizon. A separate GAO report identifies one **$47M** Louisiana transit assignment. It supplies neither a declaration key for that amount nor coverage of related missions. [DHS Inspector General testimony](https://web.archive.org/web/20170427173558id_/https://www.oig.dhs.gov/assets/TM/OIGtm_MJ_022807.pdf), [GAO preliminary observations](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-06-442t).

GAO reproduced the March 29, 2006 weekly report on Katrina, Rita and Wilma. Its agency table shows **$8.4975B in FEMA-reported mission-assignment obligations** and **$660.8M in reported expenditures**. The two values are accounting stages for the same multi-hurricane scope. The federal appropriations, performing-agency expenditure and obligation measures in the surrounding report represent additional accounting definitions. [GAO funding report, table 2](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-06-834).

GAO also documented Katrina-specific FEMA obligations to DOD of approximately **$2.2B** on September 28, 2005. The reported amount fell to **$1.7B** after an amendment on November 3 and to **$1.1B** on March 15, 2006. DOD incurred **$481M** in its own accounts in roughly the same period, a different measure. Separately, the Senate report describes a **$1B** logistics ceiling for Louisiana and Mississippi and **$100M** in reimbursement sought for work under it. These series substantiate large revisions and billing differences. Their coverage and overlap prevent treating them as additive, finalized declaration totals. [Dated revisions](results/dod_revisions.csv), [GAO account](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-06-834), [Senate report](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CRPT-109srpt322).

## Declaration evidence and model eligibility

The Senate report identifies individual assignment forms and dates for historical major declarations **DR-1603-LA** and **DR-1604-MS**. Those keys match the authenticated historical roster. The Senate citations refer to forms in the committee's evidence collection. The public text supplies no complete signed balances or reversal histories for either declaration. The later GAO cohort of 83 closed noncatastrophic disasters derives from the Disaster Financial Status Report database. Its disaster costs include program spending and administration along with mission assignments. The published sample supplies aggregate cost estimates and cannot populate the target ledger. [Exact references](results/exact_declaration_references.csv), [historical cost analysis](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-08-301).

Zero eligible 36-month declaration labels passed. Further rehabilitation would require FEMA's dated action and amendment extracts for the exact declaration identifiers, signed amounts and reversals, complete agency coverage, and a known publication and closeout cutoff. The Senate evidence citations and GAO's source description of the weekly Disaster Relief Finance Report identify possible custody routes. A partial weekly or agency series can document revisions and cannot safely classify an individual declaration into a funding band. [Gate summary](results/gate_summary.csv).
