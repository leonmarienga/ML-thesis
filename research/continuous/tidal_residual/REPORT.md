Sandy Hook contributed 48 of 72 hours and Naples contributed 42 of 72. Those gaps remain missing. The matched persistence diagnostic held the most recent available non-tidal residual constant across one or six hours. Those horizons contain 852 and 792 matched timestamp pairs. The storm rather than the timestamp is the unit of storm-level generalization. The data do not establish whether an equivalent preliminary observation was accessible for past decisions. The largest observed-minus-predicted value across matched hours was 2.79 m at The Battery.
