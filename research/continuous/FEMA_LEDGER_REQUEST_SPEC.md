# Researcher specification for restricted historical FEMA Mission Assignment records

Request a custodian-provided, disclosure-safe electronic export for the FEMA major declarations DR-1603, DR-1604, DR-1786, DR-1791 and DR-4020. Restrict scope to declaration-specific Mission Assignment (MA) accounting records and associated action histories. If available, request DR-4085 and DR-4086 as postcoverage control exports under the same extraction logic. Exclude the reserved FY2025 cohort and all associated outcomes. Ask FEMA's Office of the Chief Financial Officer or the responsible Mission Assignment records custodian to identify the archival system, coverage and limits and route older material through the applicable records process. This is a specification for the researcher.

| Requested element | Minimum usable fields |
| --- | --- |
| Declaration and episode keys | FEMA disaster number and declaration date, state or territory, incident title, master incident or related declarations, and definitive allocation by declaration where missions span multiple declarations. |
| Assignment identity | Stable MA ID, parent and superseded IDs, tasked federal department and agency, MA type and authority, and original source system or archival reference. |
| Signed action and versions | Original signed obligation, supplemental changes, partial and full deobligations, transfers, cancellations, reversals and closeout actions. Provide one row per posted action, version, effective or posting time, approval or signing date, signed native USD transaction amount or a flag indicating cumulative balances, and status codes. Preserve predecessor and parent linkage and agency splits so that actions can be deduplicated. Provide role or authorization indicator rather than personally identifying officer details. |
| Document proof | The executed official MA form and amendment or equivalent archived forms and orders, indexed by the action key. Accept digital certifications or redacted copies with action identifiers, authorization role, dates, amount, scope and status preserved. |
| Reconciliation | Separate direct federal assistance, federal operations and administrative components where available. Identify incomplete scopes, non-MA transfers, cancellations and unresolved adjustments. If bills, reimbursements, disbursements or authorizations are supplied, keep them as separately typed fields. Record cross-declaration attribution and unresolved records. |
| Time and coverage | State whether record dates refer to effective, posting, entry or public release. Provide extraction date and known late entry revisions or archival gaps. State explicitly whether agency coverage can support a declaration-wide 36-month signed net through declaration time plus 36 months, with 90 further days for timely accounting or documented revisions. Provide an exact first reliable coverage date and schema change history. |
| Export and provenance | Provide UTF-8 CSV or equivalent structured export, definitions and code lists, row counts by declaration and agency, extraction criteria, hashes or archive accession identifiers, and available declaration totals for reconciliation. Preserve anonymous stable keys if suppression is required. |

FEMA's earlier form documentation identifies FEMA Form 010-0-8 as an MA obligation record with an amendment number. Request the historical form or equivalent record in effect at the time. FEMA policy defines deobligation as a reduction to a previously recorded obligation. An independently documented signed negative posting is necessary to subtract a downward adjustment unless the custodian provides certified ordered balances with a complete reconciliation. [Historic form](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media_wysiwyg/ma_-_fema_form_010-0-8.pdf), [FEMA Mission Assignment policy](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_policy-104-010-3_mission-assignments_2024.pdf).

At intake, preserve raw files under controlled access. Test key uniqueness, signed versus cumulative semantics, duplicate supplements, one-to-many disaster attribution, missing agency rows, zero reconciliations, timeframe and source coverage. An accounting zero requires a verified complete in-scope ledger or a certified complete absence of in-scope actions. An action-free response with unknown coverage stays unavailable. Recipient reports, economic losses, appropriations and payments describe separate endpoints. The thesis will retain record-level independent episode groups and report eligibility before any historical comparison. [Prior exact-ID gate](legacy_ledger_gate/REPORT.md).
