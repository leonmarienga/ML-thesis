# Batch 2 source evidence protocols

Batch 2 pursues decision-time publication evidence and a separate observed-impact gate. Neither arm uses FY2025 funding or changes either historical benchmark. The source screens precede any modeling choice.

## A2. Federal Register publication timing

Enumerate FEMA Notice metadata from the official Federal Register API for October 2018 through September 2024, ending before FY2025. Restrict all document-level retrieval to original presidential major-declaration notices whose exact FEMA DR identifier is in the archived, eligible 376-case FY2019–24 roster. Use document docket IDs or exact DR references in the abstract. Separate amendments and corrections from original notices. Keep duplicates and unresolved mappings visible. Require exact declaration-day agreement between the Federal Register date text and the historical declaration roster before using a timing result.

Recover the public-inspection filing time from the official document's filed date and time notation. Interpret the notation in Eastern time with daylight-saving rules. Under 44 U.S.C. §§ 1503–1504, filed documents are available for public inspection, and the filing notation is published with the document. A posting URL timestamp provides a diagnostic only. The documented filing is one publication route. It leaves the actual Presidential signing time, earlier FEMA public releases, and delivery to a given responder unresolved. [Federal Register public-inspection process](https://www.archives.gov/federal-register/public-inspection), [44 U.S.C. § 1503](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t44/s1503/).

The historical declaration fields retain dates without the issue instant. Compute conservative elapsed-time bounds over the declaration civil date in Washington, D.C. Classify each horizon as covered, late, ambiguous, missing or right censored. The horizons are declaration time and plus 24, 48 and 72 hours, and 7, 14 and 30 days. Flag the September 2024 endpoint and account for missing right-edge publication. Confirm that matched source text is usable for an availability audit. Fit a forecasting arm only with material certified early coverage and legal, geospatial, and timestamp scope. Limit work to 30 index pages and 400 exact matched XML responses for this batch.

## B2. Independent earthquake impacts

Acquire the public NOAA NCEI Significant Earthquake Database years 1998 through 2024. Audit year, country, dates, spatial coordinates, stated component definitions, missingness, and units for reported deaths, destroyed buildings and economic losses. The records describe a selected consequential-event catalogue. Absence can reflect selection and reporting, and never implies zero loss or need. Distinguish event-total fields from earthquake-only fields which omit some compound consequences. Preserve the source distribution and separate numeric observations from damage orders.

Relate the authentic impact catalogue to the 1998–September 2024 ComCat earthquake source roster in batch 1B. Match unique UTC event day and epicenter within 50 km with reported source magnitude difference no more than .5 when both values are available. Resolve timestamps within a matched unique day and spatial match where both sources have adequate clock values, using a fixed 30-minute bound. Record exclusions and require unambiguous links. A damage field is an independent observation only when its provenance supports that interpretation. The global and regional domain distinctions and differing building exposures require separate gates. Feasibility requires at least 20 independent eligible training events and six historical held events for a prospective-scale comparative test, with a consistent endpoint and a validated physical predictor. If the match lacks support, preserve the real impact evidence and identify the specific source or scope that could unlock a valid model. [NOAA source and documentation](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/natural-hazards/tsunamis-earthquakes-volcanoes/earthquakes).
