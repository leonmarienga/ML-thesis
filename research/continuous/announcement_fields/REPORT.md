# Documentary program fields and early administrative geography

The 91 original White House records with a recorded modification time within the conservative T+72 window include 41 with explicit affirmative assistance language for individuals, 78 for eligible governments and 90 for hazard mitigation. Seventy-seven of the 91 were also inside the 24-hour bound. Silence and ambiguous words were retained as unresolved rather than converted to negative program flags. [Evidence excerpts, timestamps and hashes](results/field_evidence.csv).

The exact named-place screen found 1,080 county-equivalent declaration links across 84 cases. All 1,080 appear in the recovered full archived FEMA designation scope. Only 683 appeared in the prior processed geography. The recovery resolved 397 discrepancies in 64 cases. The 25 named county matches for the historical Helene declaration in North Carolina illustrate the difference. The prior processed scope matched only 10 of those names. The complete retrospective administrative archive may include territories and later additions. Neither snapshot measures physical footprint or service need. [Complete page audit](../complete_geography/REPORT.md).

The underlying records are White House archived texts and their recorded modification clocks. They do not authenticate field-by-field content at signing. The overlap of those field records with the elapsed 36-month plus 90-day funding gate is zero in every FY2019–24 outer training origin. The archive dates start in 2021 and occur too late to populate mature prior training under these tests. Earlier official original-release versions and comparable observed labels are needed before running this combined version-aware model.
