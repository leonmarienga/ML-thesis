# Recipient-side archives and exact historical assignment references

Eleven public routes produced ten distinct PDFs. The source set includes the consolidated DHS Inspector General evaluation, all four published component audits, the NASA Inspector General's full audit and semiannual summary, and three archived chapters of the House report. The historical Wayback PDF copies preserve exact original URLs. The NASA full report and selected CBP report pages required OCR. The retained OCR sidecars preserve the passages used in this audit alongside the original PDF hashes. [Protocol](../BATCH9_PROTOCOL.md), [sources](results/source_manifest.csv), [verified passages](results/verified_records.csv).

The consolidated DHS evaluation found 167 assignments with **$754.6M** in authorized authority across four DHS components and three hurricanes. It identified approximately **$29M** in documentation issues with payment support. The CBP component described 11 assignments and **$45.8M** in authorized authority across the covered Gulf Coast response. The Coast Guard and ICE reports addressed 57 and 76 assignments, respectively. These recipient-side files identify procurement, expenses and billing problems. They cannot be added to the GAO aggregate or treated as complete FEMA balances for a declaration. [DHS consolidated audit](https://www.oig.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/assets/Mgmt/OIG_09-89_Jul09.pdf).

The National Communications System audit explicitly identifies **1604DRMSNCS0900** and **1603DRLANCS0600**, linked to Mississippi and Louisiana major disaster proclamations. An agency response identifies **$100,000** remaining obligated on the Mississippi assignment and an October 2008 directive to close it. A closure directive does not establish the date or amount of a signed offset in the FEMA ledger. The response describes the Louisiana assignment and amendments totaling **$1.6M** and says the subtasked assignment had closed. These details are restricted to particular assignments and the recipient's view. [Exact assignment keys](results/exact_assignment_keys.csv), [NCS component report](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513155854id_/https://www.oig.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/assets/Mgmt/OIG_09-23_Feb09.pdf).

NASA's report shows **$541,500** in initial authority across four mission assignments and **$1,288,675** billed by March 29, 2007. Its separate **$12.6M** interagency agreement represents another legal instrument. One NASA assignment closed with zero recipient costs, a concrete example of why recipient expenditure and declaration-level obligation cannot share a zero label. The House logistics chapter gives **$19.3B** in broader Katrina-related obligations across several programs, with **$4.4B** listed for technical and direct federal assistance. Neither is a dated, complete assignment series. [NASA full audit, table 1](https://oig.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/ig-07-018.pdf), [House logistics chapter](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu/katrina/logisticsandcontracting.pdf).

The 13 verified passages yielded two newly identified assignment keys and zero eligible declaration labels. The audit identifies specific signed forms to seek, together with the need for FEMA ledger entries, linked amendments, enacted deobligations, and complete coverage through the same 36-month endpoint.
