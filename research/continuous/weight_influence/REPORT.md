# Frozen 20× signed-obligation specialist

The archived fixed 20× configuration reproduced the complete original 135-case predictions. It reached $291.80M tail MAE and $27.95M overall MAE, compared with $347.66M and $29.84M for real-only. The paired 95% bootstrap for the original tail difference is −$143.28M to +$2.50M. The fixed classifier's uncalibrated 0.10 review threshold flagged 11 of 11 tail cases and also flagged 39 other cases. The same threshold on the real-only arm flagged eight tail cases and 13 others. Brier loss rose from .056 to .104. Fixed amount improvement therefore gives insufficient evidence for adopting a review threshold or supplying calibrated risks. [Matched metrics](results/metrics.csv), [original episode bootstrap](results/original_bootstrap.csv).

Excluding the largest, second-largest and third-largest eligible earlier episode by signed dollar amount produced tail improvements of $21.62M, $54.27M and $63.78M against corresponding real-only refits. Ranking and exclusion were training-only, with unchanged input allowlists, comparators and target definitions. When an earlier fold lacks the requested rank, its unchanged predictions remain in the summary, and the absence is recorded in the exclusion ledger. Equal episode weighting retains the same direction. Removing the largest eligible earlier group shifts the overall difference from a $1.89M improvement to a $0.45M deterioration. The preregistered diagnostic reused all 135 examined cases and supplies no independent outcome evidence. [Omission ledger](results/omitted_groups.csv), [paired differences](results/paired_differences.csv).

Preserve the fixed 20× candidate for a separately versioned human-review comparison. A future model gate can require comparable mature independent signed observations, documented issue-time features and separate review and dollar evaluation. Before observing those new outcomes, the researcher should fix comparisons, activation policy, allowable false alerts, overall-error tolerance, and uncertainty criteria. The evaluation must assess catastrophic interval coverage separately. The current coverage notebooks and legacy request specification remain in force.
