# Batch 4 protocols for original announcements and observed tropical wind

## A4. Archived White House announcements

The Federal Register route is too late for early forecasts. Audit the official Biden White House archive's pre-October 2024 original major-disaster announcement pages. Use its published post sitemaps, filter by dated URL and original approval title before fetching pages, and cap direct articles at 400. Keep Open Graph and JSON-LD publication metadata only when their offset-aware times agree. A published-time field is documentary evidence of the archive's claimed initial publication. It cannot independently prove the earliest access time or when later-edited body text became visible.

Bind announcement and historical declaration using a unique state or territory and named incident start date, the archive publication date and the document's own Today or Yesterday statement where present. Require exact incident identity and publication near the recorded declaration date. Keep uncertain bindings and missing time evidence separate. Classify T0 through T+30 using the same conservative elapsed-hour logic and Eastern civil date assumption as the Federal Register audit. Repeat early checks for any pages that also report a modification timestamp after publication. Separate timestamp coverage from the field-level version status. The original document year and archive snapshot remain historical-only. [National Archives presidential website collection](https://www.archives.gov/presidential-libraries/archived-websites).

## B4. Operational tropical wind contours

Investigate time-stamped archived NHC forecast/advisory wind radii and independently observed marine station winds. The archive and buoy paths directly tested returned HTTP 403 from the execution environment. Check documented alternate official access and broadcast archives and the physical sample support before opening a new matched evaluation. A forecast wind contour is the quadrant extent of a one-minute surface wind threshold. An 8-minute buoy or 2-minute land observation is a separate verification quantity. Retain missing winds, offsets and direction and station-level observability and limit any physical claim to observed sites. An eligible experiment must use the original issued forecast and independently recorded subsequent wind without reconstructing forecasts from final best tracks. Separate forecast timing, quadrant geometry and measured units before simulating or translating into exposure. [NHC product specification](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/aboutnhcprod.shtml), [NDBC measurement definitions](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/obsdes.shtml).

### Alternate official source comparison

The NHC ATCF public archive exposed official forecast rows and NOAA CO-OPS exposed measured six-minute station winds. Use three Gulf landfall hurricanes specified before reading station outcomes. These are Laura (2020 AL13), Ida (2021 AL09) and Ian (2022 AL09). Compare forecast valid times from August 25–28, 2020, August 28–31, 2021 and September 27–30, 2022, respectively. Use 24- and 48-hour nominal lead times, complete 34-kt official wind radii and latest official station metadata. Select up to 12 stations per storm from those within 350 km of a forecast center using minimum forecast-center distance and station ID. The only outcome screen is the availability of quality-flagged winds near the forecast-valid timestamp. Use one closest eligible observed sample within ±15 minutes, with a tie going to the earlier timestamp.

Compare the published four-quadrant extent with a circular equal-area radius derived from the same four values. Use WGS84 distance and bearing and NOAA's reported sustained wind in metres per second. The stations sample only monitored points, with measurement height, averaging and point-to-area differences. Report contingency measures and missing observations by storm and lead. The purpose is to validate a constrained forecast-based hazard geometry and quantify the simple physical approximation's error. Stakes and sample support restrict this to a physical verification pilot. No FEMA obligations, human impacts or measured footprints enter the fitted or verified quantities. [ATCF archive](https://ftp.nhc.noaa.gov/atcf/archive/), [CO-OPS API](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/).
