# Blind, fixed-lead river-stage feasibility screen

**Result: strict public-forecast gate blocked.** No held-event USGS values were requested, no paired outcomes or skill estimates were computed, and no FEMA data were accessed. These three pre-2025 flood episodes are distinct from each other and from Michigan Bar December 2022 and Montpelier July 2023. The feasible next narrower direction is to validate archived RFC guidance as guidance under an explicitly different estimand, or establish exact public-product times for a future preregistered cohort.

## Frozen design and metadata-only corrections

The original outcome-blind plan is preserved, not rewritten, in `protocol.json` and `protocol-frozen.sha256`. Three episodes were purposively chosen from independently documented storm occurrences, not stage residuals or recoverable forecast accuracy: [July 2022 eastern Kentucky training storms](https://www.weather.gov/jkl/July2022Flooding), [April 2024 upper Ohio widespread flooding](https://www.weather.gov/pbz/April2nd3rd2024FloodingandSevereEvent), and [September 2024 Helene](https://www.weather.gov/media/gsp/TropicalEventSummary/PSHGSP_2024AL09_Helene_Summary.pdf). No claim of population representativeness or previously unseen events for all thesis components. Each episode is one unit even though it contributes eight potential rows: 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC across two named UTC event days. The common prespecified lead is [1,440,1,800) minutes; this is a fixed **band**, not exactly 24 hours or adjusted for the remaining variation. No replacing sites or times after candidate failure.

Two metadata corrections retain the named physical site, fixed USGS number, dates and lead rule. NOAA's [Wheeling](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/WLGW2) is **WLGW2 → 03112500**; the initially transcribed [WHLW2](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/WHLW2) means Pike Island Lock and Dam **03111520** and was not substituted. [Asheville AVLN7](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/AVLN7) is Lower Mississippi RFC (**KORN**, not the initially assumed KALR). [Jackson JKNK2](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/JKNK2) remained unchanged. The precise primary gage-height series in feet were verified using [USGS time-series metadata](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/ogcapi/v0/collections/time-series-metadata): parameter 00065, instantaneous statistic 00011. The acquisition script reads metadata and drops live NOAA status fields. These are precise contemporary crosswalks and period-of-record metadata, not proof of exact approved sample presence, datum continuity, or station location continuity on a given event date. Site-local stage is not elevation, discharge, inundation, harm, or an obligation.

## Exact clock evidence, and why it does not unblock the public claim

The [IEM NWS text archive](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/nws/text.php) supplied all 29 stage traces with intact WMO UTC day/hour/minute headers, SHEF creation timestamps, site and six-hour declarations. All were RFC guidance products with a public-distribution disclaimer pointing to the Weather Forecast Offices. No reserved HGIFE public-stage record was present. This is a property of the sampled products, not a universal statement about SHEF HGIFE. The [NWS SHEF manual](https://www.weather.gov/media/mdl/SHEF_CodeManual_5July2012.pdf) documents the code, not public publication. A text record retrievable today is not proof that its RFC WMO minute was the historical public-release time.

The separately processed but not institutionally independent [IEM HML forecast product](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/cgi-bin/request/hml.py?help=) returned 583 `Stage,ft` forecast rows with 29 archived issue vintages. A secondary exploratory archive check, conducted before any outcomes, linked traces only if at least eight overlapping valid points agreed exactly in stage and there was one HML trace within 15 minutes of both RFC clocks. There were 23 uniquely corroborated issue traces: 7 KY, 4 OH, 12 Helene. The later WMO, SHEF and corroborating HML time was used for the exploratory lead band, not asserted to be the first public release. The most recent eligible trace for a target is selected without looking at any measured data. Exact equal clock conflicts are withheld. All 24 planned targets, including 13 with no corroborated trace in the fixed band, are recorded in `fixed-lead-dispositions.csv`.

Examples of public archive copies (not proven as-issued WFO public forecasts), with issue time / conservative band lead:

- [KTIR FGUS51, KY RVFKTY](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/p.php?pid=202207270913-KTIR-FGUS51-RVFKTY), July 27 09:13 UTC; HML 09:17; target July 28 12:00 UTC, 1,603 min.
- [KTIR FGUS51, OH RVFOHW](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/p.php?pid=202404031054-KTIR-FGUS51-RVFOHW), April 3 10:54 UTC; HML 11:00; target April 4 12:00 UTC, 1,500 min.
- [KORN FGUS54, Helene RVFGSP](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/p.php?pid=202409260700-KORN-FGUS54-RVFGSP), September 26 07:00 UTC; HML 06:58; target September 27 12:00 UTC, 1,740 min.

The separate public-text probe read six IEM date-bounded FLW/FLS product feeds (all HTTP 200) from the responsible Weather Forecast Offices and retained only WMO headers and a forecast-paragraph presence flag when the exact site was in the hydrologic VTEC segment. It identified 12 site-matched warning/statement sections: 5 Jackson, 1 Wheeling, 6 Asheville. Examples of actual public WFO warning copies are [Jackson, July 28 19:19 UTC](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/p.php?pid=202207281919-KJKL-WGUS43-FLWJKL), [Wheeling, April 3 16:10 UTC](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/p.php?pid=202404031610-KPBZ-WGUS41-FLWPBZ) and [Asheville, September 26 16:44 UTC](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/p.php?pid=202409261644-KGSP-WGUS42-FLWGSP). This confirms WFO broadcasts existed, but the test does **not** establish same-issue, same-value, same-valid-time identity of a full RFC trace with any public WFO forecast. Merely matching the site or event cannot promote RFC/HML rows to strict public forecasts.

## Continue without contaminating the holdout

1. Recover an official archived NWPS/AHPS site hydrograph publication artifact or exact WFO numeric site record with issue minute, unambiguous UTC valid time and same local stage/datum. Resolve correction/version order. Compare evidence with the frozen band, not peaks or valid times selected after the event. Mark unavailable rows; don't infer publication time from HML processing or a natural-language flood warning's issue for a different stage/valid time.
2. Alternatively, define and freeze a **separate RFC operational-guidance estimand**, with explicitly limited availability claims and validation denominator, before opening any held series. For a stronger generalizable result, separately preregister additional event groups before querying values; these 3 purposive events would still only be an exploratory pilot. No threshold, parameter, model or rule tuning on these events.
3. `python -B plan_phase2.py` emits exact approved-USGS acquisition URLs only after at least two independently selected event groups have verified public-stage matches. The current dry run emits zero requests. In a later authorized phase, the series must have matching site, raw USGS 00065/00011, ft, `Approved`, unqualified, numeric, and exactly the locked valid UTC times; fail closed for ambiguous datum, multiple series, missing values, pagination, or revisions. No interpolation or early observation baseline unless an independently available issue-time gauge vintage exists. Record outages/missingness. Report signed errors and MAE by episode, and an equal-episode macro summary only as descriptive; do not treat 6-hour records as independent, claim improvement without a valid comparator, or connect this endpoint to funding.

## Replay

No non-standard packages needed. Three changed-corpus tests (missing guidance flag, contrary HGIFE stage code, undecodable trace) were rejected by `python -B test_gate.py`. The strict disposition is therefore based on verified corpus properties, not carried forward silently if the archives change. Run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS && python -B audit.py && python -B plan_phase2.py > phase2-plan.json && sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`. Source URLs, content digests, HTTP statuses, exact forecast-only sections and retained metadata accompany the audit. The *_probe network scripts are optional refetches for source or metadata only; operational WMO products may embed operational readings, which were not used; segments outside the selected forecast traces were discarded, and `kind=forecasts` was explicit in HML queries. USGS observation endpoints were never called. No remote writes.
