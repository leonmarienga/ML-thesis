# Dated local-stage reference audit: Jackson, Wheeling, Asheville

**Result: strong dated documentary evidence for Jackson and Asheville; Wheeling remains unresolved.** This is an outcome-blind metadata audit of [preregistered RFC guidance study 780999b](https://github.com/leonmarienga/ML-thesis/tree/780999b3409a96190d2d281862d2d7eb23783dc4/research/followup_20261128/rfc_guidance_stage_prereg), not a verification of the historical stage readings. No locked USGS reading was requested or scored, no preregistered selection/status was changed, and the public forecast gate remains 0/24. An unresolved event still prevents the preregistered primary three-event summary. No FY2025 or FEMA funding records were requested.

The [NOAA HADS KY](https://hads.ncep.noaa.gov/USGS/KY_USGS-HADS_SITES.txt), [WV](https://hads.ncep.noaa.gov/USGS/WV_USGS-HADS_SITES.txt) and [NC](https://hads.ncep.noaa.gov/USGS/NC_USGS-HADS_SITES.txt) files currently pair all three LIDs with the exact USGS sites. These are recent crosswalks, not dated gage-zero evidence on their own. The NGS orthometric check uses the US survey foot and a NAD83(1986) horizontal assumption for grid evaluation. NOAA grid uncertainty is not a survey alignment tolerance. USGS [datum guidance](https://water.usgs.gov/water-resources/memos/memo.php?id=4449) distinguishes physical local zero and geodetic elevation. In particular, VERTCON also does not exactly reproduce Jackson's later NAVD88 metadata (697.15 vs 697.7 ft); the separate event-date NOAA and USGS evidence in the same old reference is what supports Jackson, not retrospective geodetic subtraction.
