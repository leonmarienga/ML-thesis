# Frozen fixture design, November 28, 2026

Status: authored before first execution as a reproducibility fixture, not publicly time-stamped or preregistered. It does not replace Batch 14 or the planned participant study.

- Purpose: check a two-stage relief-allocation governance pattern under the pinned Batch 14 environment, with an explicitly **scripted human-rule proxy**, an optimization-only arm, and the same optimizer with a scripted veto. No participant behavior, treatment effect on people, or operational superiority can be inferred.
- Generator: pinned Batch 14 world and USVI CASPER source-audited demand anchors; historical obligations and FY2025 absent from these inputs. Use response/recovery × two generator families, 12 unseen simulated draws per design cell at stock ratios .55/.72/.90; preserve the pinned formula for the stock regime schedule. Precompute all 48 scenario seeds using offsets 748000, 796000, 848000, 896000 plus 1000×phase index + 100×family index + zero-based case. Check seed identities do not overlap the pinned Batch 14 registry. These are new generator draws, not independent real events or unseen world assumptions.
- At each dispatch only current demand, stock, road capacities, arrival factors and handling cap may enter a policy. The pinned world simulator returns stage-two realized state and forecasts; evaluation must not deliver these to first-stage decision logic. Stage-two policy receives only newly revealed current inputs and carryover from its own first-stage dispatch. Do not consume planning outlooks or outcome labels. Actual stage-level arrival factors are assumed known by the fixture, which may overstate what decision makers would know in practice.
- Arm 1 uses the pinned proportional dispatch (an equal-fraction heuristic over currently reachable and stocked request cells with no numerical optimizer). Arm 2 uses the pinned linear program to maximize vulnerability-weighted delivered service with zero floor. Arm 3 proposes the exact same LP and, using only the information at that dispatch, rejects it if minimum coverage over eligible zones is more than 0.10 below the heuristic's coverage. When rejected, use the heuristic; otherwise retain the LP. Eligible-zone denominators include currently reachable, stocked demand only for this immediate veto calculation. Do not repair or tune the threshold after seeing results.
- Run all arms on identical scenario realizations; calculate overall served share, high-vulnerability-zone served share, and the least-served-zone share across **all** nonzero-demand zones (closed routes remain in denominators). Verify every dispatch against current feasibility with pinned checks. Count vetoes separately by stage. Export case-level means and pairwise paired differences. Use 4,000 bootstrap resamples with replacement within the four design cells (fixed seed 20261128): illustrative scenario-resampling intervals, not confidence intervals about operational effects or participant decisions.
- Verification: input source hashes, no duplicated seed keys, identical scenarios across arms, first-stage policy noninterference under a replaced stage-two world, retained versus vetoed decisions equal the correct underlying arm at the same state, all constraints, metrics in [0,1], and deterministic replay. Record all outcomes, including a mixed or harmful hybrid outcome. No selection of an arm for deployment.
