# Day 1 convective outlook clock feasibility

Before downloading rows, select May 1 inclusive to May 8 exclusive by product issuance UTC in 2012, 2018 and 2024, calendar probes spanning the historical period; not selected from funding, tornado outcomes, known FEMA episode severities or error. Analyze only IEM processed NOAA SPC Day 1 convective forecast polygon metadata. No join to declarations, no event outcome/obligation read, no geography or forecast skill result.

Group the polygon rows by UTC valid start/end, product issuance and IEM cycle designation. Quantify distinct product-clock signatures, equality or disagreement of valid-start and issuance, noncanonical signatures, and repeated valid windows. At each top-of-hour cutoff inside each window, compare lexicographically latest valid-start/issuance among available valid windows selected (1) if valid time alone were mistakenly treated as a sufficient clock and (2) enforcing issuance no later than cutoff. Complete-case interpretation: query begins at May 1, so do not infer no active outlook at left boundary when older issued rows are outside sampling frame. Validity uses [start,end). Keep CYCLE separate: no canonical-only filter. A historical issue time in an archive is candidate nominal evidence, not proof the same geometry was consumed or first disseminated then. Forecast probabilities and risk categories are not realized severity, impact, need or funding.
